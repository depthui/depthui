# Code of Conduct

We want Depth UI to be a welcoming place for everyone, whatever their background or experience.

**Be kind.** Be respectful and patient. Assume good intent. Give feedback on the work, not the person.

**Not acceptable:** harassment, insults, personal attacks, discrimination, sexual or violent language or imagery,
publishing other people's private information, or sustained disruption of discussions.

Maintainers may remove comments, commits and issues that break these rules, and may block people who repeat them.

**Reporting.** Email the maintainers at the address on the [akram6t](https://github.com/akram6t) GitHub profile, or
open a private security advisory on this repository. Reports are handled in confidence.

This policy is adapted from the [Contributor Covenant](https://www.contributor-covenant.org), version 2.1.
